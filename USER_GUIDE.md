### *The 100% Private, Offline Office Suite for Android*

Office One was built from the ground up to respect your privacy: it requires **zero internet access**, **no registration or accounts**, and **no invasive storage permissions**. Everything you do stays 100% on your device.

## 1. Installing Office One on Your Android Device

Office One is distributed as a standard Android Package (`.apk` file). Because it is not downloaded through the Google Play Store, Android will simply ask for your one-time permission to install it.

### Step-by-Step Installation:
1. **Transfer or Download the APK**:
   * Download `OfficeOne.apk` to your phone via your web browser, Google Drive, or transfer it from your computer via USB cable.
2. **Open the File**:
   * Open the **Files** app on your phone, navigate to your **Downloads** folder, and tap `OfficeOne.apk`.
3. **Allow Installation**:
   * If prompted with *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*:
     1. Tap **Settings** on the prompt.
     2. Toggle **Allow from this source** to **ON**.
     3. Press the **Back** button.
4. **Confirm Install**:
   * Tap **Install**. In a few seconds, the app will be installed. Tap **Open** to launch Office One!

## 2. Why Office One Requests Zero Permissions

Unlike other office apps that demand access to your contacts, camera, location, and photos, **Office One asks for ZERO permissions**:

* **No Internet Access**: The app cannot connect to the internet. Your sensitive personal documents, financial spreadsheets, and confidential notes can never be uploaded or leaked to cloud servers.
* **No Storage Permissions**: Office One utilizes Android's secure **Storage Access Framework (SAF)**. When you open or save a file, you use Android's built-in file picker. Office One can only access the specific document you choose to open, and nothing else on your phone.

## 3. The Home Screen

When you launch Office One, you will see a clean, modern dashboard:

* **Quick Actions (Top Cards)**:
  * **New Document**: Creates a blank Word document (`.docx` or `.odt`).
  * **New Spreadsheet**: Creates a blank spreadsheet workbook (`.xlsx` or `.ods`).
  * **New Presentation**: Creates a blank slide deck (`.pptx` or `.odp`).
  * **Open File**: Opens the Android system file browser to select any file from your Internal Storage, SD Card, Downloads, or Google Drive.
* **Recent Documents**:
  * Displays files you have recently worked on with color-coded icons:
    * 🔵 **Blue**: Word documents (`.docx`, `.odt`, `.txt`, `.rtf`)
    * 🟢 **Green**: Spreadsheets (`.xlsx`, `.ods`, `.csv`)
    * 🟠 **Orange**: Presentations (`.pptx`, `.odp`)
    * 🔴 **Red**: PDF files
  * Tap any file to resume working immediately.
  * Tap the three dots (`⋮`) next to any recent file to share it or remove it from the recent list.

## 4. Using the Word Processor

The Word Processor offers an intuitive desktop-style **Ribbon toolbar** tailored for touchscreen navigation.

### The Ribbon Tabs:
* **Home Tab**:
  * **Text Styles**: Bold (**B**), Italic (*I*), Underline (<u>U</u>), Strikethrough.
  * **Font Controls**: Change font size (increment/decrement) and text color.
  * **Paragraph Formatting**: Align text (Left, Center, Right, Justified), bullet lists, and numbered lists.
  * **Headings**: One-tap formatting presets for *Title*, *Heading 1*, *Heading 2*, and *Normal* text.
* **Insert Tab**:
  * Insert page breaks, tables, pictures, and hyperlinks.
* **Layout & View Tabs**:
  * Check word count, character count, and zoom in/out.

### Saving and Exporting:
* **Save**: Tap the 💾 **Save** icon at the top right to commit your edits back to the file.
* **Save As / Export to PDF**: Tap the menu (`⋮`) and select **Export as PDF** to generate a print-ready document.

## 5. Using the Spreadsheet Editor

The Spreadsheet Editor provides a responsive data grid and calculation engine compatible with Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets.

### Key Capabilities:
* **Grid Navigation**: Tap any cell (e.g. `A1`, `B4`) to select it. Drag corner handles to highlight cell ranges.
* **Formula Bar**: 
  * Displays the exact formula of the selected cell.
  * Type numbers, labels, or formulas starting with an equals sign (`=`).
* **Supported Formulas**:
  * **Math & Aggregates**: `=SUM(A1:A10)`, `=AVERAGE(B1:B5)`, `=COUNT(C1:C20)`, `=MAX(A1:A5)`, `=MIN(A1:A5)`, `=PRODUCT(A1:A2)`, `=ROUND(A1, 2)`.
  * **Logical**: `=IF(A1>50, "Pass", "Fail")`, `=AND(A1>0, B1>0)`, `=OR(A1>0, B1>0)`, `=NOT(A1)`.
  * **Lookup**: `=VLOOKUP(101, A1:D20, 2, FALSE)`.
  * **Text**: `=CONCATENATE(A1, " ", B1)`, `=LEFT(A1, 3)`, `=RIGHT(A1, 2)`, `=UPPER(A1)`, `=LOWER(A1)`.
* **Multi-Sheet Workbooks**:
  * Tap the tabs at the bottom (`Sheet 1`, `Sheet 2`) to switch between sheets.
  * Tap `+` to add a new worksheet.

## 6. Using the Presentation Editor

Create and rehearse slide decks on the go.

* **Slide Canvas**: Tap on slide text boxes to edit titles, bullet points, and subtitles.
* **Slide Organizer**: The left-hand panel shows visual thumbnails of all your slides. Tap any slide to jump to it, or tap **Add Slide** to insert a new slide layout.
* **Presenter Notes**: Tap the collapsible **Notes** bar at the bottom to write presentation cues and speaker notes.
* **Fullscreen Slideshow**: Tap the ▶️ **Play** button on the top bar to preview your slides in full-screen presentation mode.

## 7. Using the PDF Reader & Annotator

Office One includes a standalone PDF reading and markup engine built on Apache PDFBox.

* **Pinch-to-Zoom**: Fluid zooming from 50% to 400% for fine text and diagrams.
* **Search in Document**: Tap the 🔍 **Magnifying Glass** to find phrases across pages.
* **Annotations & Highlights**:
  * Tap the **Highlight** chip to highlight key paragraphs.
  * Tap the **Sticky Note** chip to attach comments.
* **Page Management**:
  * Rotate misaligned pages 90° clockwise.
  * Delete unnecessary pages.
  * Merge multiple PDF files into one, or split chapters into separate PDFs.

## 8. AutoSave and Crash Protection

Losing work due to a dead phone battery or an accidental app swipe is frustrating. Office One includes two automated safeguards:

1. **AutoSave (`AutoSaveManager`)**:
   * Every 30 seconds, your working document is saved to a secure internal recovery file.
   * If your phone shuts down unexpectedly, reopening Office One will automatically display an **"Unsaved Changes Found - Restore?"** banner.
2. **Atomic Backups (`.bak`)**:
   * When saving, Office One first creates a safe backup copy (`document.bak`). If power is lost mid-save, the original file is never corrupted and can be restored immediately.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Can I open files sent to me on WhatsApp or Gmail?**
> **A:** Yes! Tap on any `.docx`, `.xlsx`, `.pptx`, or `.pdf` attachment in WhatsApp, Gmail, or your browser. Android will display Office One in the "Open with" menu.

**Q: Does Office One show ads?**
> **A:** No. Office One contains zero advertisements, zero tracking code, and zero telemetry analytics.

**Q: Does Office One work when Airplane Mode is turned on?**
> **A:** Yes. The application is 100% offline and never requires internet access.

**Q: How do I transfer files to my computer?**
> **A:** Since documents are saved in standard Android folders (like Downloads or Documents), simply plug your phone into your computer via USB, or share them via Bluetooth or email.
